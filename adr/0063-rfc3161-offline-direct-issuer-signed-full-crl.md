# ADR 0063 — Offline issuer-signed full CRL for directly root-issued RFC3161 signing leaf

Status: Accepted conservative **pilot revocation coverage slice**, 2026-11-16; [#94](https://github.com/KeyserDSoze/TemporalMark/issues/94) child of still open [#15](https://github.com/KeyserDSoze/TemporalMark/issues/15). **Not a complete historical qualified-trust or multi-hop PKIX revocation validator.**

## Prior architectural context and normative basis

Reviewed ADR [0002](0002-qualified-vs-delegated-timestamping.md), [0052–0062](0062-rfc3161-live-gen-time-and-issuance-time-x509-path.md), [TSA operator guide](../docs/TSA_HTTP_TRANSPORT.md), [threat model](../docs/THREAT_MODEL.md), [roadmap](../docs/ROADMAP.md), `ExternalRfc3161TimestampProvider`, `ExternalRfc3161CertificateChainPolicy`, API DI/config and X.509 disposable root/intermediate/signer tests. #92 validates the actual CMS signer path to a dedicated operator-approved `CustomRootTrust` CA root; #93 explicitly binds `VerificationTime` to a bounded signed `TSTInfo.genTime`. However `X509Chain` explicitly sets `RevocationMode.NoCheck`: neither path nor signed genTime tells whether the signer was revoked. `X509Chain.RevocationMode=Offline` alone depends on potentially incomplete, environment-dependent cached data and is not a substitute for a demonstrably supplied issuer CRL.

[RFC 5280 §5.1](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5280#section-5.1) defines signed CRL DER `CertificateList`, inner/outer signature algorithm identifier, issuer, `thisUpdate`/`nextUpdate`, positive serial numbers, revoked entries and extensions. [RFC 5280 §6.3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5280#section-6.3) describes the importance of checking issuer signature, scope and status. .NET 10 [CertificateRevocationListBuilder.Build](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/system.security.cryptography.x509certificates.certificaterevocationlistbuilder.build?view=net-10.0) permits genuine RSA issuer-signed disposable CRL positive/negative fixtures. Its `Load` method only **decodes**, not independently verifies issuer signature or service trust; do not accept a decoded CRL unverified.

## Deliberately narrow, fail-closed decision

1. Require a **server-controlled** `TimestampProvider:IssuerCrlBase64Der` when an external TSA URL is set, in addition to the existing reviewed root, signer SHA-256 leaf pin and reqPolicy OID. Accept only canonical standard Base64 for **one complete DER CRL**, max 1 MiB. Reject absent/malformed/noncanonical/trailing DER, issuer CRL missing mandatory nextUpdate, unsupported algorithms or extension semantics **before HTTP POST** at syntax layer. No tenant-sourced value, DNS/AIA/CDP/CRL network fetch, system trust-root fallback or change to disabled/no-TSA operation.
2. After verifying RFC3161 DER/CMS signature/imprint/nonce, critical-only TSA EKU, reviewed leaf pin, signed policy, live bounded signed genTime, and **the actual verified CMS signer chain** to the operator-approved root: enforce a **direct root→signer chain only**, and reject any issuer intermediate. This is explicitly conservative because a multi-hop PKIX path needs **revocation for each non-root certificate** and multiple matching CRLs (or independently qualified OCSP), not just one configured root CRL. Keep the underlying #92 path checker able to test multi-hop paths on its own, but make a delegated issuance with multi-hop + single CRL fail until the next staged policy.
3. Verify the DER CRL is a **complete, direct issuer CRL**, not delta/indirect, with exactly matching DER issuer X.500 DN to the signer issuer and reviewed CA root subject, CA root KeyUsage `CrlSign`, and **RSA PKCS#1 v1.5 SHA-256 issuer signature** over exact signed `TBSCertList`. Require inner and outer signatureAlgorithm parameters agree and only known algorithm; reject any unknown critical extensions or unsupported entry extensions and delta/indirect. Require `thisUpdate <= signedGenTime < nextUpdate` as an explicit *current-snapshot coverage* policy; a later-issued retrospective CRL is deliberately **not accepted**. Check positive serial as actual certificate `SerialNumber` and **reject signer if listed anywhere**, even if its revocationDate is after genTime (conservative; avoids falsely declaring good status in historical/invalidityDate ambiguity). No stale list fallback; operators must update the reviewed CRL before it expires.
4. Keep `X509Chain` at `RevocationMode.NoCheck` for its *path-only* role, and document clearly that a **separate, explicit offline CRL check** now covers exactly the direct root-issued leaf after chain validation. Do not misrepresent NoCheck as native CRL enforcement. No support for RSA-PSS, ECDSA, indirect/delta CRL, reason-partitioned CRL or multiple hops in this first slice; **reject**, not silently skip. A valid imported CRL does not authenticate the QTSP's service qualified status, prove historical revocation beyond this specific window, guarantee comprehensive CRL distribution point coverage, or preserve evidence for future revalidation.
5. Both Azure and on-prem require the same operator-managed CRL DER config. Operationally obtain signed CRL from an independently authenticated CA channel, confirm appropriate distribution point/scope and freshness, rotate with audited change control; never trust arbitrary DNS/CDP endpoints. Retain `Unverified` pilot status, no CAB/AgID legal gate reduction. #15/#17/#13 remain open.

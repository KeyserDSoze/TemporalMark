# ADR 0031 — Two-hop offline MDS-signing-chain CRL *test* with no authorization

Status: **accepted only for TDD cryptographic regression on an untrusted test PKI**, 2026-11-16. Focused [#60](https://github.com/KeyserDSoze/TemporalMark/issues/60) child of #57/#55, after ADR 0025–0030 and #58/#59. Production reviewer issuance remains blocked by V015/V016; suspended registrar CLI, #44/#50/#46/#38 remain OPEN.

## Threat model, standards and source review

RFC 5280 §6.1 and §6.3 distinguish **certification-path validity** from **certificate revocation checking**. A verified end-entity signature or a fresh CRL for the end-entity alone cannot establish whether its **issuing intermediate CA was revoked**. RFC 10007 (2026) clarifies the necessity of validating the CRL issuer's certificate path and observing cRLSign authorization where present. For direct-issued certificates, one needs the issuer-signed CRL for **each** non-root certificate, not just the last leaf.

FIDO's August 2026 MDS [root rollover](https://fidoalliance.org/mds-changelog/) changed GlobalSign R3 to **R46**; an R3→R46 cross-certificate appears in `x5c[2]` since 2026-08-31 and expires 2029-03-18 together with R3. The pinned Fido2 4.3.3 metadata repository has an R3 hardcoded root and divergent CRL-handling code paths. ADR 0029 `ReviewerMdsJwsSignatureCandidateVerifier` only verifies cryptographic JWS/chain **without revocation**; ADR 0030 `ReviewerMdsOfflineCrlCandidateVerifier` validates a real signed DER CRL **only for a direct leaf**. Neither component is a genuine approved FIDO MDS trust service.

## Deliberate design and hard boundaries

1. Create an independent, **unwired** `ReviewerMdsOfflineTwoHopCrlCandidateVerifier.Inspect` for **exactly three X509 v3 certificates**: test leaf, test intermediate CA and explicitly supplied test root CA, with precisely two **externally supplied DER X509 v2 full CRLs**: the intermediate's issuer-signed CRL for the leaf, and the root's issuer-signed CRL for the intermediate. No network, certificate downloads, issuer URL, HTTP/CLI/API or SQL inputs.
2. Use .NET 10 `X509Chain` with `CustomRootTrust`, `NoFlag`, and `DisableCertificateDownloads=true`; require **exactly three chain elements in the supplied order**, and byte-exact root and intermediate equality. `X509RevocationMode.NoCheck` is allowed **only** for this certificate-signature path construction, **never** as evidence that a certificate is not revoked. **Then** separately verify each signed CRL and the exact certificate serial using the pinned BouncyCastle.Cryptography 2.7.0. Reuse the existing #59 CRL parser/signature/date/extension/revoked-serial logic through an **internal shared verifier** instead of reimplementing cryptography. CA issuing and `cRLSign` permissions are checked for both root and intermediate; leaf must have DigitalSignature and must NOT be a CA.
3. Fail closed on **either** CRL absent/expired, forged/wrong signer or issuer, revoked leaf or intermediate, untrusted root, mismatched/swap path, unknown CRL critical/delta/indirect, unsupported signature or unbounded input. No partial acceptance, no root CRL needed for its self-signed trust-anchor certificate (whether a root is independently trustworthy is **outside** this slice).
4. A successful synthetic test chain returns **only** `UNAPPROVED` and `TWO_HOP_CRL_ONLY_ROOT_UNTRUSTED` plus two SHA-256 CRL digests; no raw certificate, CA material, signed metadata BLOB, trust bit or activated signer. The root CA is **caller selected** and may be adversarial; passing this test therefore **does not** prove FIDO MDS R46 trust, GlobalSign provenance, CAB authorization, non-revocation outside the snapshot, or trusted time. Do not connect to MDS JWT, attestation status, approval or SQL execution.
5. TDD on direct `main`: ADR before RED xUnit; generate disposable RSA root→intermediate→leaf and real issuer-signed CRLs at test runtime; assert positive only `UNAPPROVED` and negative revoked intermediate, revoked leaf, swapped/missing/tampered/expired CRL, wrong/counterfeit issuer or root, missing cRLSign and unknown extensions. Four GitHub CI jobs SUCCESS **at exact final main HEAD** before closing #60.
6. **Remaining #57/#55 release blockers:** independent R46 root distribution/rotation and audit, real FIDO signed JWT x5c **including cross-signed R3/R46 transition**, real signed/current CRLs for **all** certificate hops with CDP/AKI scope, root and CRL version monotonic persistence, air-gapped/on-prem/Azure signed imports, revocation/PKI outage and time-drift controls, trust-anchor custody, OCSP/delta/delegated issuers where necessary, model-specific AAGUID attestation path, CAB/DPIA and two-person enrollment governance. #57/#55/#53/#44/#50/#46/#38 all stay OPEN.

## Deployment, qualification and privacy

No Azure-only dependency, internet reachability, production root bundle, test keys in source control or change to SQL grants. Apply same safety freeze on-prem and Azure. Avoid logging full CRLs or X.509 certificate identity values. Nothing here demonstrates a qualified electronic timestamp under eIDAS, WORM evidence, attestation of physical device ownership or a right to activate registered WebAuthn credentials.
